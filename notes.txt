# SIGNAL UNKNOWN

**Category: Sci-Fi / Mystery / Thriller**

---

## BEGINNING

### Scene 1: The Signal

It is 2047.

For the first time in human history, a signal has been detected coming from outside the Solar System.

It isn't random noise. It repeats every 17 minutes and 42 seconds.

The signal has been named **UNKNOWN-01**.

You are part of the first team assigned to investigate it.

After several days of analysis, your team finally manages to isolate a small section of the transmission.

The room goes quiet as the computer processes the data.

Three words appear on the screen:

**"DO NOT ANSWER."**

Nobody speaks for several seconds.

Your supervisor looks around the room.

"Well?"

### Choices

**1. Continue decoding the signal.**
`decodeSignal`

**2. Stop the experiment.**
`stopExperiment`

**3. Send a simple response.**
`respondSignal`

---

# Scene 2A: Continue Decoding

You continue processing the signal.

The computers work for several hours before another section becomes readable.

A new message appears:

**"IT HEARS YOU."**

Before anyone can react, the signal changes again.

A sequence of numbers begins repeating.

Your computer identifies them as coordinates.

You enter them into the astronomical database.

Nothing appears.

No planet.

No star.

No known object.

Just empty space.

### Choices

**1. Investigate the coordinates.**
`investigateCoordinates`

**2. Continue decoding the signal.**
`decodeMore`

**3. Tell the public about UNKNOWN-01.**
`tellPublic`

---

# Scene 2B: Stop the Experiment

You shut down the equipment.

The room becomes silent.

Then one of the monitors turns back on.

Nobody touched it.

A message appears.

**"WHY DID YOU STOP?"**

The signal is still being received.

Your equipment isn't.

### Choices

**1. Restart the equipment.**
`restartEquipment`

**2. Shut down the entire facility.**
`shutdownFacility`

**3. Respond to the message.**
`respondMessage`

---

# Scene 2C: Send a Response

You type:

**"WHO ARE YOU?"**

The message is transmitted.

Nothing happens.

One minute passes.

Then two.

The signal changes.

Your computer begins receiving data at a speed the system wasn't designed to handle.

Images begin appearing.

Star systems.

Planets.

Unknown structures.

Something resembling a map.

Then everything stops.

A final message appears:

**"YOU HAVE BEEN SEEN."**

The signal disappears.

---

# Scene 3: The Coordinates

You decide to investigate the coordinates.

Your team compares them with astronomical records.

There should be nothing there.

But when the coordinates are plotted against historical observations, something strange appears.

The coordinates form part of a trajectory.

The path passes through several locations in space before ending at the UNKNOWN-01 coordinates.

The path appears to begin near Earth.

Nobody can explain why.

### Choices

**1. Search historical records for anything related to the trajectory.**
`searchRecords`

**2. Send a probe toward the coordinates.**
`sendProbe`

**3. Keep the discovery classified.**
`keepClassified`

---

# Scene 4: The Old Mission

The search through historical records produces something unexpected.

A classified mission from 2021.

Officially, it was a communications experiment.

Three astronauts were sent beyond Earth's immediate orbital region.

The mission was supposed to last eleven months.

It never returned.

The official report states that contact was lost.

But buried inside the archived files is a recording that was never released.

You play it.

Static.

Then a voice.

**"We found something."**

A long pause.

**"It knows we're here."**

The recording ends.

### Choices

**1. Find out where the ship disappeared.**
`findShip`

**2. Search for the astronauts' identities.**
`findAstronauts`

**3. Examine the rest of the mission records.**
`examineRecords`

---

# Scene 5: The Missing Transmission

You uncover more fragments from the 2021 mission.

The astronauts had detected something.

They didn't know what it was.

Their reports describe unusual signals, strange movements in deep space, and an object they couldn't identify.

Then the final transmission appears.

The audio is damaged.

You manage to recover one sentence:

**"It isn't alone."**

The recording cuts out.

At almost the exact same moment, UNKNOWN-01 begins transmitting again.

Your computer displays a new sequence.

It isn't a message this time.

It's coordinates.

### Choices

**1. Follow the new coordinates.**
`followCoordinates`

**2. Compare them with the 2021 mission.**
`compareCoordinates`

**3. Disconnect from UNKNOWN-01.**
`disconnectSignal`

---

# Scene 6: The Connection

The coordinates from UNKNOWN-01 match the location where the 2021 mission disappeared.

Your team goes silent.

This can't be a coincidence.

You compare the original signal with the old mission's recordings.

They contain the same frequency.

UNKNOWN-01 isn't a new signal.

It has been transmitting for at least 26 years.

Then you notice something else.

The signal isn't coming from the coordinates.

The coordinates are being **sent by the signal**.

Someone, somewhere, wants you to look there.

### Choices

**1. Send a probe.**
`sendProbe`

**2. Attempt to communicate again.**
`contactSignal`

**3. Search for more information about the 2021 mission.**
`searchMission`

---

# Scene 7: The Discovery

The probe reaches the coordinates.

At first, it finds nothing.

Then its sensors detect an object.

It's small.

Artificial.

And completely unlike anything humanity has ever built.

The probe approaches.

Its camera activates.

The object suddenly changes position.

Not quickly.

Instantly.

The feed cuts to static.

Then the camera comes back.

The object is gone.

But something else is visible.

A planet.

It wasn't there before.

Your team checks the astronomical data.

There is no record of a planet in that location.

### Choices

**1. Send the probe closer.**
`approachPlanet`

**2. Bring the probe back.**
`recallProbe`

**3. Try communicating with the object.**
`contactObject`

---

# Scene 8: The Message

Before anyone can make a decision, the signal changes.

For the first time, it isn't repeating.

It's communicating directly.

A sentence appears:

**"YOU FOUND IT."**

Your supervisor asks:

"Found what?"

The computer begins displaying images.

The strange object.

The unknown planet.

The 2021 spacecraft.

Then something else.

Earth.

A photograph of Earth taken from space.

The image is dated:

**2047.**

The exact date is today.

Nobody speaks.

### Choices

**1. Ask how it got the image.**
`askImage`

**2. Ask what the object is.**
`askObject`

**3. Ask what it wants.**
`askIntent`

**4. Disconnect everything.**
`disconnectAll`

---

# FINAL SECTION

Whatever choice you make, the signal responds.

But the response isn't an explanation.

It's another set of coordinates.

This time, they're much closer.

They're inside the Solar System.

Then another message appears:

**"WE NEED TO SHOW YOU SOMETHING."**

The transmission ends.

Your team is left with a decision.

### Choices

**1. FOLLOW**
Send a spacecraft to the new coordinates.
`followSignal`

**2. WAIT**
Continue studying the signal before doing anything else.
`waitAndStudy`

**3. RESPOND**
Ask the sender exactly what it wants to show you.
`askWhat`

**4. SHUT DOWN**
End all communication with UNKNOWN-01.
`shutdownSignal`

The decision is yours.
